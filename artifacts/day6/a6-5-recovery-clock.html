How to play

  1. Build TeaLeaf's backup plan: where the copies go, how often, and whether you test.
  2. Watch the 3-2-1 check: 3 copies, 2 kinds of storage, 1 copy away from the office.
  3. Then run two disasters, a ransomware attack and a fire, and see what you lose.

TeaLeaf's target: lose no more than one day of orders, and be down no more than one day.

Copy 1 is always the live data on TeaLeaf's server. Where do the extra copies go?
