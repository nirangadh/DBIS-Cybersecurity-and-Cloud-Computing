How to play

  1. Read each TeaLeaf risk.
  2. Choose its likelihood (how likely?) and its impact (how bad?), from 1 to 5.
  3. See it on the risk matrix, and compare with the model answer. Within 1 point counts as right.

Risk score = likelihood x impact. Ask one question to keep them apart: chance or damage?

Likelihood: how likely is it?

Impact: how bad would it be for C, I or A?
