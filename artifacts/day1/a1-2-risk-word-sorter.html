A risk sentence has all three parts: a threat, a weakness, and harm.

Help: the three risk words
Threat
Something or someone that can cause harm.
Vulnerability
A weakness that a threat can use.
Risk
The chance that a threat uses a weakness and causes harm. Look for all three parts.
