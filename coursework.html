Coursework

Six short in-class tests make up half of your module mark.

The LMS is the official source. Submission links, dates, marks and any changes are on the LMS. If anything on this page differs from the LMS, follow the LMS.

What counts

  • Coursework: 50% of the module mark, from six short in-class tests.
  • Exam: 50%, a 2-hour written paper after the module. See Session 11.14.

The in-class tests

  • When: Days 1 to 6, from 3:00 to 4:00. Day 7 has no test.
  • What: two questions. Question 1 is about that morning; question 2 is about that afternoon.
  • Only that day: every question comes from that day's main ideas. "Good to know" points are never tested.
  • Kind of question: a short TeaLeaf situation. You decide something and say why.
  • Already practised: each question asks for the same kind of decision as a game you played that day, in a new situation.

What you do, step by step

  1. Write (35 minutes): answer both questions on paper. No books, no phones.
  2. Photo (5 minutes): take clear photos of your pages and make one PDF.
  3. Upload (10 minutes): upload the PDF to the LMS. File name: DBIS11_StudentID_DayN.pdf, for example DBIS11_StudentID_Day3.pdf.
  4. Hand in (5 minutes): give your paper to the lecturer before you leave.

How it is marked

  • Each question is marked from 0 to 5.
  • We mark your ideas, not your grammar. Simple English is fine.
  • A clear decision with a good reason gets full marks. Where more than one decision is sensible, any well-reasoned answer can get full marks.
  • Your best 5 of 6 tests count, so one missed or weak day does not cost you. Check the LMS for how missed tests are handled.
MarkWhat it looks like
5A sound decision, a clear reason, the day's key words used correctly, applied to TeaLeaf
4A sound decision and reason; key words used loosely
3A reasonable decision, but the reason is weak or general
2A decision with no reason, or partly wrong
1A relevant attempt with major errors
0Blank or not relevant

How to prepare

  • Play that day's games again before 3:00, especially the Words to know ladder.
  • Read the Check yourself questions at the end of that day's notes.
  • Use the deciding questions for look-alike words.
  • Try the practice questions and the glossary.
  • Write your own answers. Copying or sharing answers is not allowed.
