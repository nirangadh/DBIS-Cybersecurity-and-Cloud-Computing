Glossary

Every word from the Words to know lists, with the day and session where you learned it.

81 words from Days 1 to 6.

ACDEFHILMOPRSTVW#

A

Accept
Keep a small risk and live with it Day 6, Session 11.11
Access control
Deciding who can open what. Always the customer's job Day 5, Session 11.9
Accountability
The diary: tracing who did something, later, from records Day 2, Session 11.4
Asset
Anything that has value to the business Day 1, Session 11.1
Attacker
A person or group who harms our information, on purpose or by mistake Day 1, Session 11.2
Authentication
Proving who you are, for example with a password and a code Day 2, Session 11.4
Authenticity
The door: checking that it is real, now (really you, or really from them) Day 2, Session 11.4
Authorisation
What you are allowed to do after you log in Day 2, Session 11.4
Availability
Keep it available: we can use it when we need it Day 1, Session 11.1
Avoid
Stop the risky activity, so the risk is gone Day 6, Session 11.11

C

Cloud
Renting computing over the internet, and paying for what you use Day 4, Session 11.7
Cloud provider
The company that rents out the cloud Day 5, Session 11.9
Confidentiality
Keep it secret: only the right people see it Day 1, Session 11.1
Consent
A person's clear agreement to their data being used for a reason Day 5, Session 11.10
Contain
Stop an incident from spreading Day 6, Session 11.12
Control
Anything that reduces a vulnerability Day 3, Session 11.5
Grows on Day 6: anything that reduces a risk, by preventing, detecting or fixing Day 6, Session 11.11

D

Data breach
Information seen or taken by the wrong people Day 1, Session 11.2
Personal data lost, stolen, or seen by the wrong people Day 5, Session 11.10
Data subject
The person the personal data is about Day 5, Session 11.10
Deepfake
A fake voice or video made by AI to copy a real person Day 2, Session 11.3
Denial of service
Flooding a website so real users cannot use it Day 1, Session 11.2
DNS
The internet's phone book: finds the IP address for a name Day 3, Session 11.5

E

Encryption
Turning a message into nonsense that only the right key turns back Day 3, Session 11.6

F

Firewall
The guard at the gate: blocks connections that are not allowed Day 3, Session 11.5

H

Hashing
Making a fingerprint of data. Any change gives a new one Day 3, Session 11.6
Hidden cost
A cost that you did not expect when you signed up Day 4, Session 11.8
HTTPS
The padlock: data is encrypted and the site's name is checked Day 3, Session 11.6
Hybrid cloud
Public and private cloud, joined together Day 4, Session 11.7

I

IaaS
Renting the machines. You look after everything on them Day 4, Session 11.7
Impact
How bad the damage would be, from 1 (tiny) to 5 (severe) Day 6, Session 11.11
Incident
A risk that has become real: something has gone wrong Day 6, Session 11.12
Insider
Someone inside the business: staff, past staff or partners Day 1, Session 11.2
Integrity
Keep it correct: nobody changes it wrongly Day 1, Session 11.1
IP address
A number address that tells data where to go Day 3, Session 11.5

L

Layers of defence
Many controls together, because one is never enough Day 3, Session 11.5
Likelihood
How likely a risk is to happen, from 1 (rare) to 5 (almost certain) Day 6, Session 11.11
Lock-in
When it is very hard or costly to move to another provider Day 4, Session 11.8

M

Malware
Bad software made to cause harm Day 1, Session 11.2
MFA
Logging in with two or more different kinds of factor Day 2, Session 11.4
Misconfiguration
Setting something up wrongly, or leaving it open by mistake Day 5, Session 11.9

O

On-premises
Your own computers, in your own building, that you buy and look after Day 4, Session 11.7

P

PaaS
Renting a ready place to build and run your own app Day 4, Session 11.7
Packet
A small, numbered piece of data Day 3, Session 11.5
Passphrase
A password made of several random words Day 2, Session 11.4
Password manager
An app that makes and remembers strong passwords for you Day 2, Session 11.4
Pay as you go
Paying only for what you use, each month Day 4, Session 11.8
PDPA
Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 Day 5, Session 11.10
Personal data
Any information that can point to a person, directly or indirectly Day 5, Session 11.10
Phishing
A fake email sent to many people to trick them Day 2, Session 11.3
Private cloud
Cloud used by one organisation only Day 4, Session 11.7
Private key
The only key that unlocks. Never shared Day 3, Session 11.6
Public cloud
Cloud shared by many customers, run by a big provider Day 4, Session 11.7
Public key
Like an open padlock: anyone may have it; it only locks Day 3, Session 11.6
Public Wi-Fi
Wi-Fi that many strangers share Day 3, Session 11.5

R

Ransomware
Locks files and asks for money Day 1, Session 11.2
Malware that locks your files and asks for money to unlock them Day 6, Session 11.12
Reason (motive)
Why an attacker does it Day 1, Session 11.2
Reduce
Add a control, so the risk is smaller Day 6, Session 11.11
Right of access
A person's right to see what data a business keeps about them Day 5, Session 11.10
Right to erase
A person's right to ask for their data to be deleted Day 5, Session 11.10
Risk
The chance a threat uses a weakness and causes harm Day 1, Session 11.1
Risk matrix
A 5 x 5 grid that places risks by likelihood and impact Day 6, Session 11.11
Risk owner
The person who watches a risk and acts on it Day 6, Session 11.11
Risk register
A table of risks: each with an owner, a score and an action Day 6, Session 11.11

S

SaaS
Renting a finished app that you just use Day 4, Session 11.7
Scaling
Growing or shrinking how much you rent, when you need to Day 4, Session 11.8
Security policy
A short list of security rules that everyone must follow Day 5, Session 11.10
Shared key

Shared responsibility
The provider looks after the cloud; the customer looks after what it puts in it Day 5, Session 11.9
Smishing
Phishing by text message (SMS, WhatsApp) Day 2, Session 11.3
Social engineering
Tricking people into giving information, money or access Day 2, Session 11.3
Spear phishing
A fake message made for one person, using real details Day 2, Session 11.3
Spyware
Secretly watches and steals information Day 1, Session 11.2

T

Threat
Something or someone that can cause harm Day 1, Session 11.1
Transfer
Move the cost to someone else, for example an insurer Day 6, Session 11.11
Trojan
Pretends to be useful, hides harm inside Day 1, Session 11.2

V

Virtual machine
A pretend computer that runs inside a real one Day 4, Session 11.8
Virus
Spreads with files that people share Day 1, Session 11.2
Vishing
Phishing by phone call Day 2, Session 11.3
VPN
A private tunnel for data travelling outside the office Day 3, Session 11.5
Vulnerability
A weakness a threat can use Day 1, Session 11.1

Worm

3-2-1 rule
3 copies of data, on 2 kinds of storage, with 1 copy away from the office Day 6, Session 11.12
